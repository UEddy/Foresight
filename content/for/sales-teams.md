## What is competitive intelligence for sales teams?

Strip the jargon and it comes down to one thing: knowing what your competitors are doing, and knowing it in time to use it. Market research studies a whole category and takes weeks. A competitive intelligence platform usually gets built for a product marketing analyst who lives in dashboards. Neither helps the rep who is about to get asked, on a call in ten minutes, why your product costs more than the company down the street. That rep needs a straight answer about what changed and how to handle it, and nothing else. That is the job Nivaria does. It watches each competitor's public pages, notices when something moves, and writes the change up with a threat rating and a suggested response. No portal to log into. No analyst to chase.

## Why do reps ignore most competitive intelligence?

Ask around and you hear the same story. Someone built a competitor wiki. There was a shared doc, maybe a set of playbook pages. For about three weeks people used it. Then it went stale, nobody kept it current, and reps went back to winging it. The information was real. It was just parked somewhere nobody opens during a live call. And the stakes keep climbing. Crayon's [2026 State of Competitive Intelligence](https://www.crayon.co/state-of-competitive-intelligence-2026) found that 57.5% of teams say more of their deals are competitive now than a year ago. You feel that in the pipeline. A document you have to remember to check cannot keep up. What works is boring and mechanical: put the update where the rep already is, write it the way they would say it out loud, and refresh it without anyone lifting a finger.

## How does Nivaria give sales teams intel they will use?

Everything lands where the rep already works. A tracked competitor cuts a price or ships a feature, and a brief shows up in Slack, Discord, or your inbox, whichever you picked. The brief opens with the change, not with preamble. It rates how much the change matters, then hands you a few talking points and a suggested response you can drop into an email or say on the call. Hook up Google Calendar and you also get a heads-up before any meeting that mentions a competitor you track, so nobody has to prep. Need to send outreach? It drafts the message in your voice. The whole thing is built to be read in the sixty seconds a rep has between calls, because that is honestly all the time anyone gives it.
