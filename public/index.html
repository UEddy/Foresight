The problem

A competitor repriced on Tuesday. Your rep found out from the prospect on Friday, in the room where the deal was lost.

By the time a competitor's move reaches your team through the pipeline, the deal has already been reframed without you. That gap is where forecast slips, and nobody logs it as a competitive loss.

Competitor tracking and monitoring features

  • Competitor website monitoring Daily automated checks on pricing pages, feature pages, homepages, and announcement pages.
  • Severity classification Every detected change is scored high, medium, or low threat, with the reasoning shown.
  • Automated battlecards Each meaningful change becomes a battle card with talking points and a recommended response.
  • Slack and Discord alerts Briefs land in the channel your revenue team already works in, minutes after detection.
  • Pre-meeting briefings Connect Google Calendar and get a condensed competitive brief before a meeting with that account.
  • Outreach playbook drafts Subject lines and email bodies generated in your own voice profile, ready to send or edit.
  • Competitor change timeline Replay every move a competitor made, in order, with 12 months of history on Pro.
  • Revenue Impact Dashboard Log competitive win/loss against the changes that touched each deal.
  • CRM sync Planned Push competitor context onto the account record in HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive.

Nivaria monitors and analyzes publicly available information such as website content, pricing pages, product announcements, messaging changes, and other publicly accessible business signals. We do not access private accounts, bypass authentication, or collect non-public data.
